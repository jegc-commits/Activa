ACTIVA (Activation of Clubs and Institutional Workshops for Academic Life) is a mobile app to browse activities, enroll, take attendance and manage clubs and workshops at UTNG.

Features:
- Browse workshops by category.
- Enroll and withdraw from activities.
- Attendance control and history.
- Announcements, surveys, favorites and chat.
- Instructor and administrator panels.
- Local SQLite database, works offline.
- No ads, no telemetry.

FOSS build: no proprietary dependencies (no Google/Firebase).
